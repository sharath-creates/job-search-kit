# Role families

A family is a set of job titles that share a CV angle and a search vocabulary.
Titles inside a family are interchangeable enough that one tailored CV works
across them. Titles across families are not.

Read this during step 1 when the person's title list needs grouping. Pick at
most two.

---

## Why two

Each family needs its own summary variant, its own bullet selection, and its
own query set. Running four means each gets a quarter of the applications, and
after two months no family has the twenty-application sample the weekly review
needs to say whether it works.

Two families means a readable answer in about three weeks.

---

## The families

### Product and analysis
Product Manager, Associate Product Manager, Product Owner, Business Analyst,
Technical Business Analyst, Product Analyst, Business Systems Analyst,
Functional Consultant.

Shared vocabulary: requirements, user stories, backlog, roadmap, stakeholders,
acceptance criteria, discovery, prioritisation.

### Customer-facing technical
Technical Account Manager, Solutions Consultant, Solutions Engineer,
Sales Engineer, Implementation Consultant, Forward Deployed Engineer,
Deployment Strategist, Customer Solutions Engineer, Professional Services
Consultant.

Shared vocabulary: implementation, onboarding, integration, adoption,
escalation, scoping, technical discovery, enterprise clients.

### Software engineering
Software Engineer, Backend Engineer, Frontend Engineer, Full Stack Engineer,
Platform Engineer, Mobile Engineer, SDET.

Shared vocabulary: the specific languages and frameworks, system design,
testing, code review, CI/CD, on-call.

### Data
Data Analyst, Analytics Engineer, Business Intelligence Developer,
Data Engineer, Data Scientist, Insights Analyst.

Shared vocabulary: SQL, warehouse, pipeline, dashboard, modelling, experiment,
metric definition.

### Programme and delivery
Project Manager, Programme Manager, Delivery Manager, Scrum Master,
Technical Programme Manager, Chief of Staff.

Shared vocabulary: dependencies, milestones, risk, RAID, governance, sequencing,
cross-functional.

### Marketing
Product Marketing Manager, Growth Marketer, Content Marketer,
Demand Generation Manager, Lifecycle Marketer, Brand Manager.

Shared vocabulary: positioning, messaging, funnel, attribution, campaign,
launch, segmentation.

### Sales and revenue
Account Executive, Sales Development Representative, Account Manager,
Customer Success Manager, Renewals Manager, Revenue Operations Analyst.

Shared vocabulary: quota, pipeline, ACV, churn, expansion, discovery call,
territory.

### Design
Product Designer, UX Designer, UX Researcher, UX Writer, Design Systems
Designer.

Shared vocabulary: flows, wireframes, prototypes, usability testing, design
system, accessibility.

### Operations and finance
Business Operations Analyst, Strategy Analyst, Financial Analyst, FP&A Analyst,
Supply Chain Analyst, Process Improvement Lead.

Shared vocabulary: forecasting, variance, process mapping, SLA, cost per unit,
reconciliation.

## Grouping rules

- A title belongs to the family whose vocabulary the job descriptions actually
  use, not the one that sounds closest. Solutions Engineer sits with
  customer-facing technical even though it contains the word Engineer.
- Two adjacent titles inside one family are one choice, not two.
- When a person's list spans four families, the two to keep are usually the one
  they have the most recent evidence for and the one they want next. Say that
  out loud and let them pick.
- A family that produces nothing after twenty applications gets retired. That
  rule lives in the weekly review task and should be stated during setup, so
  the retirement later reads as the plan working.
