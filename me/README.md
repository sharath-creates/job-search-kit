# me/

Your answers live here. Everything in this folder except this file and
`EXAMPLE-profile.md` is gitignored, so your CV, your salary floor and your
email address never reach GitHub.

**Before setup, put your CV in this folder.** PDF, Word or plain text all
work, under any file name. Setup finds it on its own.

Setup writes three files here:

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `profile.md` | Who you are, what you want, what you will not accept |
| `answer-sheet.md` | Your stock answers to application form fields |
| `setup-state.md` | Which setup steps are done, and the IDs of your tasks |

If you want to start over, delete those three files and say "run setup". Your
CV can stay.
